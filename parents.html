❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Parent Inquiries Answered

Are Nimco's products suitable for kids with nut allergies?

We process roasted peanuts on dedicated lines in our facility. All packets with peanuts (like Tasty, Masala Peanuts, and Mixtures) carry clear bold allergen warnings. For nut-free preferences, our Butter Popcorn and Aloo Bhujia are manufactured in separated zones.

How do Nimco snacks maintain freshness without synthetic preservatives?

Our secret is packaging technology rather than chemicals. We utilize high-barrier metallized film pouches flushed with inert food-grade nitrogen gas. This stops moisture and oxygen from softening the snacks, keeping them naturally crisp for 6 months!

Can I place bulk orders for school birthday parties or sports days?

Yes! We have an automated School & Party Calculator on our Where to Buy / Contact page that calculates exact pack assortments and provides up to 25% bulk savings!
