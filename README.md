# Nimco's Namkeen & Sweets

> **"Khate Khate Mann Na Bhare!"**  
> 100% Pure Vegetarian | Export Quality | Traditional Indian Namkeen & Savouries

A modern, high-conversion web experience built for **Nimco's Namkeen & Sweets**, showcasing authentic product lines from the `assets/` directory with responsive presentation, 3D product showcase, interactive product filters, and commercial inquiries for distributors.

---

## Brand Architecture & Design

1. **Appetite-Led Palette**:
   - **Nimco Royal Red** (`#B81D24`): Heritage brand foundation and primary accent.
   - **Harvest Gold** (`#FEC33F`): Warm roasted corn, savory crunch, and highlight badges.
   - **Deep Charcoal** (`#181615`): High-contrast, legible typography.
   - **Ivory Cream** (`#FAF7F2`): Clean, warm background surface.
   - **Pure Vegetarian Green** (`#1E8E3E`): Vegetarian certification emblem.

2. **Typography**:
   - Modern, authoritative sans-serif pairing: **Plus Jakarta Sans** and **Poppins** via Google Fonts.

3. **Product Showcase**:
   - Dynamic 3D product slider inspired by modern FMCG experiences.
   - Comprehensive catalogue with nutrition breakdown and ingredient transparency.

## Running Locally

```bash

```

Then visit: `http://localhost:8080`
