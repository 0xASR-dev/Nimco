Rooted in Tradition, Crafted with Purity

From a small family kitchen in 1998 to a modern automated snack brand trusted by households across India, our devotion to authentic flavours remains unchanged.

"Khate Khate Mann Na Bhare"

In Indian households, tea-time namkeen is not just food. It is a moment of togetherness, conversations, and sharing. Our golden promise has always been simple: craft flavours so balanced and crisp that one handful naturally leads to another.

We select only choice pulses, sun-dried chillies, pure asafoetida (hing), and premium peanuts. Every single recipe is 100% vegetarian, free from artificial additives, and sealed under food-grade nitrogen.

Milestones in Our Journey

Key moments that built Nimco's into a beloved packaged food brand.

1998

Foundations in Traditional Namkeen

Founded with traditional hand-spun recipes, pure groundnut oils, and a dedication to serving fresh, high-quality savouries to retail tea stalls and local communities.

2008

Pioneering Affordable Single-Serve Packs

Introduced signature ₹5 retail packets across North and Western India, making authentic royal aloo bhujia and khatta meetha affordable for school students and daily commuters.

2018

Automated Packaging Facility

Modernized manufacturing with high-speed automated vertical form-fill-seal machines and nitrogen flushing to preserve crispness and shelf stability.

2026

15 Signature Lines & Nationwide Reach

Today, our catalogue comprises 15 authentic offerings including Popcorn, Besan Coated Peanuts, Moong Dal, Chana Dal, and Traditional Jeera Papad serving over a million households every month.
