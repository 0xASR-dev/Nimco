⏳ Our Milestone Trail

From Family Kitchen to Snack Galaxy

Take a walk through the golden chapters of Nimco's Namkeen & Sweets.

'98

The First Kadhai in Gujarat

Our founder began with a single copper kadhai, fresh groundnut oil, and an unshakeable belief that snacks for neighborhood kids should be pure, vegetarian, and bursting with genuine Indian warmth.

'08

The ₹5 Pocket-Money Revolution

We pioneered the ₹5 pocket-friendly packet without cutting a single corner on quality. Children could now buy authentic Aloo Bhujia and Khatta Meetha with their own small pocket money coins!

'18

Touchless Nitrogen Sealing Facility

We upgraded our kitchens into an automated facility with optical color sorters, stainless steel conveyors, and nitrogen gas packing, earning Export Quality credentials.

'26

15 Signature Flavours & Next-Gen Fun

Today, our range includes Butter Popcorn, Besan Coated Peanuts, Moong Dal, Hing Chana, and Jeera Papad, delighting over 1.2 million happy munchers every single month!
